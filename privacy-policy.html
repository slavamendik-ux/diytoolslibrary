2. What personal data we collect

We collect different data depending on how you interact with us:

  • Waitlist sign-ups: your email address, which we store in Mailchimp so we can let you know when membership opens.
  • Membership: your name, email address, phone number, and a record of the tools you've borrowed, so we can run your membership and make sure tools are returned and available for other members.
  • Payments: if you become a paying member, your card and billing details are collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe. We do not store your full card details ourselves.
  • Communications: if you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we'll keep a record of that conversation to help resolve your query and for our own records.
  • Donations and volunteering: if you offer to donate tools or volunteer with us, we'll collect the details you provide (such as your name and contact details) to arrange this.

3. How we use your data

  • To set up and manage your membership, including processing payments and tracking tool borrowing and returns.
  • To send you service-related communications, such as confirmations, reminders about tools due back, or changes to opening hours.
  • To send you marketing updates (such as news about workshops or the waitlist) — only where you've agreed to receive these, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To respond to enquiries, donations, or volunteering offers.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations, for example keeping financial records.

4. Our legal basis for processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide membership services you've signed up for, including processing payments.
  • Consent: for marketing emails and the waitlist — you can withdraw this at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to run and improve our services, keep records, and respond to enquiries, in ways that wouldn't reasonably surprise you.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to retain records (e.g. financial data) to comply with the law.

5. Who we share your data with

We don't sell your personal data. We do share it with a small number of trusted service providers who help us run DIY Tools Library CIC:

  • Mailchimp — for managing our waitlist and email communications. Mailchimp is based in the US; where your data is transferred outside the UK, Mailchimp maintains appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as required under UK GDPR. See Mailchimp's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — for processing membership payments securely. Stripe is a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. See Stripe's privacy policy.

We may also share data where required by law, for example with HMRC or law enforcement.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Waitlist data is kept until you unsubscribe, or until we've launched and either converted you to a member or the list is no longer needed.
  • Membership and borrowing records are kept for the duration of your membership, and for a reasonable period afterwards to resolve any outstanding issues (such as unreturned tools).
  • Financial records (linked to payments processed via Stripe) are retained for as long as required by UK tax law — currently up to 6 years.

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten"), where applicable.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for anything we process based on consent (like marketing emails).

To exercise any of these rights, contact Vjatseslav Mendik at contact@diytoolslibrary.co.uk. We'll respond within one month, as required by law.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. Cookies

Our website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If this changes in the future, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as our services grow. We'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page, and let you know about any significant changes.
