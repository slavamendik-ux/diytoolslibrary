2. Membership & payment

  • You must be at least 18 years old to become a member or borrow tools.
  • Membership is billed monthly according to the tier you choose. Prices and what's included in each tier are set out on our Membership page.
  • Payments are processed securely by our payment provider, Stripe. We don't store your full card details.
  • No deposit is required to join or to borrow tools.

3. Cancellation & cooling-off

  • You may cancel your membership at any time through your online account or by emailing us, with no notice period. Your membership remains active until the end of your current billing period, and you won't be charged again after that.
  • As a new member, you have a statutory 14-day right to cancel your membership immediately after signing up for a full refund, provided you haven't borrowed any tools during that period, in line with the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

4. Borrowing tools

  • The number of tools you can borrow at once, and how often, depends on your membership tier — see our Membership tiers for details.
  • The maximum borrowing period is 10 calendar days from collection, after which the tool must be returned. This limit doesn't apply to consumables you've purchased from us.
  • Late returns may incur a late fee of £2 per item per day, and repeated late returns may result in a temporary suspension of your borrowing privileges.
  • Tools are supplied without consumable accessories (such as drill bits, saw blades or sandpaper) unless stated otherwise — these can be purchased on-site.
  • Tools remain the property of DIY Tools Library CIC (or the original donor, where applicable) at all times.

5. Tool safety & your responsibilities

  • All electrical tools are PAT tested for safety before being added to the library and at regular intervals afterwards.
  • You're responsible for using borrowed tools safely, for their intended purpose, and in accordance with any instructions or guidance we provide.
  • You must report any fault, damage, or safety concern with a tool to us as soon as you become aware of it, and stop using it immediately if you believe it's unsafe.
  • Most tools in our library are second-hand or refurbished. We don't ask members for a deposit or automatic penalty to cover repair costs, and we rely on members to use borrowed tools appropriately and return them in working order where possible. We reserve the right to suspend or end membership in cases of repeated, deliberate, or reckless damage to library tools.
